\section{Purpose and scope}
This interface connects the \emph{disorder-Wannier pipeline} (archive
\texttt{deltaH.h5}) to \textsc{LinReTraCe} at Boltzmann-like cost, without
touching the Fortran core.  It produces
\begin{enumerate}
\item a \textbf{scattering HDF5} with momentum- and band-resolved
\emph{on-shell} disorder rates $\Gam_{\vk n}$, bandshifts from the
disorder mean field $\Sigma^{(1)}(\vk)$, and $Z=1$ (elastic disorder is
frequency independent);
\item a \textbf{dressed energy HDF5}: a copy of the plain
\texttt{lwann} energy file in which the transition matrix elements $M$
carry the resummed static disorder (diffuson) ladder,
$M\to\lambda M$ intra-band and analogously for inter-band elements;
\item optionally a \textbf{$\lambda$ HDF5} containing the vertex
enhancements themselves, together with the ensemble-averaged correlator
in the band basis, for physics analysis.
\end{enumerate}
\textsc{LinReTraCe} is then run \emph{unchanged} on the dressed energy
file together with the disorder scattering file: its polygamma kernels
are untouched; all vertex physics enters through the matrix elements.
Section~\ref{sec:whylambda} derives why this factorisation is
legitimate; these notes are self-contained given only the disorder
pipeline and the \textsc{LinReTraCe} paper.

\section{Background, notation, and what the archive contains}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{The archive stores configurations, not averages.}
The pipeline archive \texttt{deltaH.h5} stores, for every disorder
configuration $c$ (weight $w_c$, $\sum_c w_c=1$), the \emph{two-point}
disorder Hamiltonian in the primitive Wannier basis $\{L\}$,
\begin{equation}
\Delta H^{(c)}_{LL'}(\vk,\vk-\vq_p),
\qquad p=1,\dots,N=\det S,
\label{eq:archive}
\end{equation}
where the $\vq_p$ are the $N$ discrete momentum transfers of the
supercell's reciprocal superlattice (a supercell with matrix $S$ folds
the primitive Brillouin zone $N$-fold; disorder that is periodic on the
supercell can transfer crystal momentum only in these $N$ quanta).  In
the file this is held as real-space hopping blocks per configuration;
the class \texttt{DisorderArchive} performs the Fourier transform and
provides, \emph{computed on load},
\begin{equation}
\bar V(\vk,\vk-\vq_p)=\sum_c w_c\,\Delta H^{(c)}(\vk,\vk-\vq_p),
\qquad
\delta V_c=\Delta H^{(c)}-\bar V ,
\label{eq:dV}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the ensemble mean and the \emph{fluctuation} of the disorder
potential.  The $\vq_p{=}0$ mean is the (virtual-crystal) first cumulant
$\Sigma^{(1)}(\vk)=\bar V(\vk,\vk)$ and is folded into the band energies
(bandshifts) rather than into scattering.  \textbf{Why store
configurations rather than averages?}  Per-configuration storage is
lossless: any moment of the ensemble can be formed later --- the second
moment $\sum_c w_c\,\delta V_c\otimes\delta V_c^\dagger$ used by
Born/SCBA and by the ladder below, but also the \emph{full}
$\delta V_c$ needed by the T-matrix (engine \texttt{ata}), which resums
all higher cumulants of a single scatterer and cannot be reconstructed
from second moments alone.  Storing only averaged correlators would
close that door.  For \emph{analysis} convenience the interface exports
the transport-relevant average --- the ensemble-averaged squared
correlator rotated into the band basis on the actual $\vk$-mesh --- into
the $\lambda$ file (dataset \texttt{correlatorW2},
Sec.~\ref{sec:outputs}); caching $\bar V$ additionally inside
\texttt{deltaH.h5} would be a backward-compatible pipeline extension,
but is not required (the on-load construction is cheap and exact).

\paragraph{Linearised Green's function and band basis.}
As in \textsc{LinReTraCe}, the retarded propagator of band $n$ is
Lorentzian,
\begin{equation}
G^{R}_{\vk n}(\eps)=\frac{Z_{\vk n}}
{\eps-a_{\vk n}+i\tilde\Gam_{\vk n}},\qquad
G^{A}=\big(G^{R}\big)^{*},\qquad
A_{\vk n}(\eps)=-\tfrac1\pi\,\mathrm{Im}\,G^R_{\vk n}(\eps),
\label{eq:G}
\end{equation}
with $a_{\vk n}=E_{\vk n}-\mu$ the renormalised band energy,
$\tilde\Gam$ the renormalised scattering rate and $Z$ the quasiparticle
weight ($Z{=}1$ for pure disorder).  The band basis is obtained by
diagonalising the static Hermitian part,
$H(\vk)+\Sigma^{(1)}(\vk)$ (plus the DMFT static parts in combined mode,
Sec.~\ref{sec:dmft}); the eigenvector matrix $U(\vk)$ rotates the
velocities, $v_{nm}^{\alpha}(\vk)=[U^\dagger(\partial_{k_\alpha}
H)U]_{nm}$ (including the inter-orbital Peierls correction, exactly as
in \texttt{lwann}), and the disorder fluctuations,
\begin{equation}
\delta V^{\rm band}_{c}(\vk n\to\vk'm)
=\big[U^\dagger(\vk)\;\delta V_c(\vk,\vk')\;U(\vk')\big]_{nm},
\qquad \vk'=\vk-\vq_p .
\label{eq:dVband}
\end{equation}
The \textsc{LinReTraCe} Onsager coefficients have the generic structure
$\mathcal L\propto\sum_{\vk,n,m}\mathcal K(\vk,n,m)\,M(\vk,n,m)$ with
kernels $\mathcal K$ (polygamma functions of $a$, $\tilde\Gam$, $\beta$)
and Peierls matrix elements
$M^{\alpha\beta}(\vk,n,m)=\overline{v^{\alpha}_{nm}}\,v^{\beta}_{nm}$;
this interface modifies only the $M$.

\paragraph{Diagrammatic vocabulary.}
Disorder averaging of the current--current correlation function
organises into diagrams in which the two propagator lines
(``\emph{rails}'', one retarded at energy $\eps$, one advanced at the
same $\eps$ for DC) are connected by pairwise-correlated disorder
insertions (``\emph{rungs}'', each rung carrying one factor of the
correlator $\sum_c w_c\,\delta V_c\otimes\delta V_c^\dagger$ and one
momentum transfer $\vq_p$).  Ladders (rungs in parallel, non-crossing)
build the \emph{diffuson}; maximally crossed rungs build the
\emph{Cooperon} (weak localisation), which is \emph{not} included here
(Sec.~\ref{sec:notincluded}).

\section{On-shell rates}
\label{sec:rates}
The disorder self-energy in the Born approximation is
$\Sigma(\vk,\eps)=\sum_{p,c}w_c\,\delta V_c(\vk,\vk')\,G(\vk',\eps)\,
\delta V_c^\dagger(\vk,\vk')$.  Its band-diagonal imaginary part,
evaluated \emph{on shell} ($\eps=a_{\vk n}$, the energy at which the
state's own spectral weight sits --- the same choice
\textsc{LinReTraCe} offers for $\Gam^0_{\vk n}$), gives with the
Lorentzian rails~\eqref{eq:G}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;
\Gam_{\vk n}=\sum_{p,c,m} w_c\,
\big|\delta V^{\rm band}_{c}(\vk n\to\vk'm)\big|^{2}\,
\frac{Z_{\vk'm}\,\tilde\Gam_{\vk'm}}
{(a_{\vk n}-a_{\vk'm})^{2}+\tilde\Gam_{\vk'm}^{2}}\;}
\label{eq:rates}
\end{equation}
(the Lorentzian is the finite-lifetime replacement of the golden-rule
$\pi\delta(a_{\vk n}-a_{\vk'm})$).  Engines:
\texttt{born} evaluates \eqref{eq:rates} once with a seed width;
\texttt{scba} (default) iterates it to self-consistency,
$\tilde\Gam=Z(\Gam+\Gam_{\rm other})$ on the right-hand side --- this is
the conserving partner of the ladder in Sec.~\ref{sec:ladder};
\texttt{ata} replaces $\delta V_c\,G\,\delta V_c^\dagger$ by the full
per-configuration T-matrix $T_c=(1-\delta V_cG)^{-1}\delta V_c$ resummed
within each $\vq_p$ orbit, capturing multiple scattering off one
configuration (impurity bound-state trends) beyond second order.
The evaluation energy is selectable (\texttt{--rate-eval}): `onshell'
($\eps=a_{\vk n}$, the quasiparticle width of each state's own
resonance --- governs activated transport) or `fermi' ($\eps=0$ ---
governs the Lorentzian tail spectral weight at $\mu$, i.e.\ metals and
the low-$T$ saturation regime); keep it matched with
\texttt{--vertex-eval} so rails and ladder form one consistent
linearised pair (a warning is issued otherwise).
Elastic scattering makes $\Gam_{\vk n}$ temperature independent; the
rates are replicated over the requested $T$ axis
(\texttt{--disorder-temps}, linear, or \texttt{--disorder-temps-log},
logarithmic as in the \texttt{linretrace} config), unless
\texttt{--dmft} supplies the axis (Sec.~\ref{sec:dmft}).
\emph{Write convention}: \textsc{LinReTraCe}'s core multiplies
\texttt{scatrate} by \texttt{qpweight} on read; the file therefore
carries the bare rate (disorder-only: $Z=1$, as computed; combined:
$\gamma^{\rm QP}_{\rm DMFT}/Z+\Gam_{\rm dis}$).

\section{Why the vertex just multiplies the matrix elements}
\label{sec:whylambda}
This section is the heart of the interface; we give the complete
argument.

\paragraph{(a) Which diagrams are summed.}
The dissipative DC conductivity involves the disorder-averaged
particle--hole bubble at equal energies.  Keeping, of all averaged
diagrams, the non-crossing particle--hole \emph{ladder} built on the
same rails and the same rung as the SCBA self-energy (a conserving,
Baym--Kadanoff pair --- mixing rungs and rails from different
approximations is known to violate positivity), the RA part of the
transport function per energy $\eps$ reads
\begin{equation}
T^{RA}(\eps)=\frac1{N_k}\sum_{\vk}\Tr\big[v^{\alpha}\,
G^{R}(\vk,\eps)\,\Lambda^{\beta}(\vk;\eps)\,G^{A}(\vk,\eps)\big],
\label{eq:TRA}
\end{equation}
where the three-point vertex $\Lambda$ resums \emph{all} rung orders
through the linear (Bethe--Salpeter) equation
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;
\Lambda_{nm}(\vk;\eps)=v_{nm}(\vk)+\sum_{p,c}w_c\Big[
\delta V_c(\vk,\vk')\,G^{R}(\vk',\eps)\,\Lambda(\vk';\eps)\,
G^{A}(\vk',\eps)\,\delta V_c^{\dagger}(\vk,\vk')\Big]_{nm}\;}
\label{eq:ladder}
\end{equation}
with band-diagonal rails~\eqref{eq:G} and $\delta V_c$ from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:dVband}.  Iterating \eqref{eq:ladder} generates
$v+K[v]+K[K[v]]+\dots=(1-K)^{-1}v$: the one-rung, two-rung, \dots{}
ladders.  \emph{No double counting:} exactly one current vertex in the
bubble \eqref{eq:TRA} is replaced by $\Lambda$; since $\Lambda$ already
contains every ladder insertion \emph{between} the two currents,
dressing both sides would count each diagram twice.  Hence the dressed
matrix element is one-sided,
\begin{equation}
\tilde M^{\alpha\beta}_{nm}(\vk)
=\mathrm{Re}\big[\overline{v^{\alpha}_{nm}(\vk)}\,
\Lambda^{\beta}_{nm}(\vk)\big]
\;\xrightarrow{\;n=m\;}\;
\lambda^{\alpha\beta}_{\vk n}\,M^{\alpha\beta}_{nn}(\vk),
\qquad
\lambda\equiv\tilde M/M .
\label{eq:dressing}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{(b) Elasticity makes $\Lambda$ static.}
The rungs in \eqref{eq:ladder} carry \emph{no energy transfer}
(static disorder): both rails sit at the single external energy $\eps$,
which enters only \emph{parametrically}.  $\Lambda(\eps)$ therefore
varies on band-structure scales (bandwidth, distance to band edges and
van-Hove points, broadened by $\tilde\Gam$), whereas the
\textsc{LinReTraCe} kernels vary on the thermal scale $T$ --- the same
scale separation that justifies linearising $\Sigma(\omega)$ in the
first place.  Evaluating $\Lambda$ at one representative energy per
state (Sec.~\ref{sec:eval}) then removes the $\eps$-dependence
entirely.

\paragraph{(c) The kernel factorisation.}
With a static $\Lambda$, the energy integral of the RA bubble
factorises per $(\vk,n)$:
\begin{equation}
\int\! d\eps\,\big({-}f'(\eps)\big)\,
\big|G_{\vk n}(\eps)\big|^{2}\,
\overline{v^{\alpha}_{nn}}\,\Lambda^{\beta}_{nn}
=\tilde M^{\alpha\beta}_{nn}(\vk)\;
\underbrace{\int\! d\eps\,({-}f')\,|G_{\vk n}|^{2}}_{\textstyle
\propto\;\mathcal K_{11}(\vk,n)},
\label{eq:factorize}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ exactly the \textsc{LinReTraCe} kernel times the dressed matrix
element: \emph{the kernels are untouched, only} $M\to\tilde M=\lambda M$.
The same argument applies verbatim to $\mathcal K_{12}$ and
$\mathcal K_{22}$ (extra factors $\eps^{a+b-2}$ are scalars under the
vertex equation), so charge, Seebeck and thermal kernels share one
$\lambda$ --- the Jonson--Mahan structure survives elastic vertex
corrections.  One approximation should be stated explicitly: the
ladder is solved for the RA section, which dominates DC transport for
$\tilde\Gam$ small against band scales; the RR/AA contributions inside
the kernels (the $\psi_2$-type terms) are kept \emph{bare}.

\paragraph{(d) What is \emph{not} included.}
\label{sec:notincluded}
(i) the maximally crossed (Cooperon) channel --- weak localisation is a
separate, additive channel with its own momentum structure and
cutoff sensitivity, planned as a future extension; (ii) the
disorder-current end terms $\langle\delta v\,\delta V\rangle$ and the
$\langle\delta v\,G\,\delta v\rangle$ dumbbell, which require the
derivative data $\partial_\vk\Delta H$ (available in the full
\texttt{disorder\_transport} code, where they are shown to matter for
optical spectra; at DC they are a percent-level correction on the test
systems); (iii) electron--electron vertex corrections (excluded by the
same assumption as in \textsc{LinReTraCe}); (iv) rail-off-diagonal
(inter-band) vertex \emph{components} --- see
Sec.~\ref{sec:interband}.

\section{Solving the ladder: orbits, the Ward identity, and zero-mode
deflation}
\label{sec:ladder}
Because the rung transfers only the $N$ discrete momenta $\vq_p$,
Eq.~\eqref{eq:ladder} couples $\vk$ only to points of its
\emph{transfer orbit} $\{\vk-\sum_p n_p\vq_p\}$ (size $\le N$ on a
commensurate mesh): the solve block-diagonalises into small linear
systems of dimension $|{\rm orbit}|\times n_b^2$.

\paragraph{The per-energy Ward identity.}
For \emph{elastic} scattering, the anti-Hermitian part of the SCBA
self-energy satisfies, at every single energy $\eps$ separately,
\begin{equation}
\Sigma^{R}(\vk,\eps)-\Sigma^{A}(\vk,\eps)
=\sum_{p,c}w_c\,\delta V_c(\vk,\vk')
\big[G^{R}-G^{A}\big](\vk',\eps)\,
\delta V_c^{\dagger}(\vk,\vk') ,
\label{eq:ward}
\end{equation}
which is the statement that scattering redistributes spectral weight
\emph{within} each energy shell without creating or destroying it
(particle-number conservation per energy; it is the elastic,
energy-resolved form of the usual Ward identity relating $\Sigma$ and
the vertex).  Consequence for \eqref{eq:ladder}: at $\omega=0$ the RA
kernel $K$ possesses an eigenvalue (arbitrarily close to) $1$ at
\emph{every} $\eps$ --- the conserved-density (diffusion) mode, the
$q\to0$ precursor of the diffusion pole.  A naive solve of
$(1-K)\Lambda=v$ amplifies rounding by $1/(1-\lambda_{\max})$.

\paragraph{Why the current is orthogonal to that mode --- and when.}
The protection is a $\vk\to-\vk$ parity argument, so we must first ask
when the band structure itself obeys $a_{-\vk n}=a_{\vk n}$.
\emph{Sufficient} conditions: spatial inversion, \emph{or} time-reversal
symmetry (Kramers: for any non-magnetic system, with or without
spin--orbit coupling, $E_n(-\vk)=E_n(\vk)$ with bands sorted by energy),
or more generally any operation of the magnetic point group mapping
$\vk\to-\vk$ (e.g.\ the combined $PT$ symmetry of many
antiferromagnets).  None is individually \emph{necessary}; what fails is
the case with none of them --- e.g.\ non-centrosymmetric magnets (such
as magnetic Weyl semimetals), where $E_n(-\vk)\neq E_n(\vk)$
generically.  Given such a symmetry, the diffusion mode is \emph{even}
(built from the local spectral density, with $a$ and $\tilde\Gam$ even),
while the current source is \emph{odd},
$v^{\alpha}_{nn}(-\vk)=-v^{\alpha}_{nn}(\vk)$.  There is additionally a
\emph{weaker protection that needs no point symmetry at all}: the
solvability of $(1-K)\Lambda=v$ requires only that the source be
orthogonal to the \emph{left} zero mode, whose weight depends on $\vk$
through $(a_{\vk n},\tilde\Gam_{\vk n})$; if $\tilde\Gam$ depends on
$\vk$ only through the band energy, the overlap is a Brillouin-zone
total derivative, $\sum_\vk\partial_\vk F(a_{\vk n})=0$ --- exactly
zero.  Genuine $\vk$-anisotropy of $\tilde\Gam$ in a system without any
$\vk\to-\vk$ symmetry leaves a small residual overlap, which is
precisely what the leak diagnostic below measures.  For the correlator,
the corresponding condition is
\begin{equation}
\sum_c w_c\big|\delta V^{\rm band}_c(\vk n\to\vk'm)\big|^2
=\sum_c w_c\big|\delta V^{\rm band}_c(-\vk\,n\to-\vk'\,m)\big|^2 ,
\label{eq:parity}
\end{equation}
then $K$ commutes with the parity operator, its even and odd sectors
decouple, and the odd current source cannot excite the even zero mode:
the physical current vertex is finite.  Condition \eqref{eq:parity}
holds for a self-averaging ensemble (each configuration's mirror image
is equally likely); for small ensembles it is only approximate --- the
code measures the violation (``correlator parity asymmetry'') and
reports it.

\paragraph{Truncated-SVD deflation and the leak diagnostic.}
The solver inverts $(1-K)$ through its singular-value decomposition,
dropping directions with $\sigma<\texttt{svd\_cut}\cdot\sigma_{\max}$
(relative cut, default $10^{-2}$): this removes the near-null diffusion
direction.  For each Cartesian source the amplitude that \emph{would}
have entered the dropped directions, relative to the kept solution, is
recorded as the \emph{leak}.  Small leak $\Rightarrow$ the truncation is
harmless (parity protection intact).  Leak $\gtrsim10^{-3}$
$\Rightarrow$ the source genuinely drives the marginal mode (broken
parity, Eq.~\eqref{eq:parity} violated): $\lambda$ near the affected
energies is unreliable; enlarge the configuration ensemble.  Both the
asymmetry and the leak are printed on every run.

\section{Evaluation energy: \texttt{--vertex-eval \{onshell,fermi\}}}
\label{sec:eval}
The weight multiplying $\lambda_{\vk n}(\eps)$ in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:factorize} is $(-f'(\eps))|G_{\vk n}(\eps)|^2$: a thermal
window of width $T$ at $\mu$ times the state's Lorentzian of width
$\tilde\Gam$ at $a_{\vk n}$.  Since $(-f')$ decays exponentially while
the Lorentzian tail decays only polynomially, this weight is
\emph{bimodal} for $|a|\gg T$: a tail lobe at $\eps\simeq0$ of height
$\sim1/a^{2}$ and an on-shell lobe at $\eps\simeq a$ of height
$\sim e^{-|a|/T}/\tilde\Gam^{2}$; the on-shell lobe dominates iff
$|a|/T<2\ln(|a|/\tilde\Gam)$.  Hence two selectable prescriptions:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{fermi}: one solve per orbit at $\eps=0$.  Correct for
\emph{metals} --- there the states that carry the current have
$|a|\lesssim\max(T,\tilde\Gam)$ and both prescriptions agree to leading
order --- and for the \emph{low-$T$, tail-dominated}
(resistivity-saturation) regime of narrow-gap semiconductors, where
transport proceeds through the $\tilde\Gam$-induced in-gap spectral
weight at $\mu$.
\item \texttt{onshell}: energy-binned solves at $\eps=a_{\vk n}$ (pair
midpoints for inter-band elements; bin width
$\sim\tilde\Gam_{\min}/3$).  Correct in the \emph{activated} regime,
where the carriers live at the band edges and $\eps=0$ lies in the gap
where no state exists; and the only choice that retains vertex-induced
particle--hole \emph{asymmetry} (valence and conduction states with
different orbital character acquire different $\lambda$), which feeds
$L_{12}$ and the Seebeck coefficient.  Residual error
$O(\tilde\Gam\,\partial_\eps\ln\lambda)$, temperature independent.
\end{itemize}
The migration of the dominant lobe with temperature is precisely the
characteristic-temperature ($T^\ast$) phenomenology of finite-lifetime
semiconductors.  \textbf{Practical rule: run both settings.}  Agreement
$\Rightarrow$ the static dressing is controlled.  Disagreement
$\Rightarrow$ the system is in the bimodal crossover, where no single
static $\lambda$ is exact --- there, benchmark against the
frequency-resolved vertex of the full \texttt{disorder\_transport}
machinery.

\section{Inter-band elements}
\label{sec:interband}
\textsc{LinReTraCe}'s kernels include inter-band transitions
$\mathcal K(\vk,n,m)$ with proper degenerate limits, so the interface
dresses inter-band matrix elements as well: Eq.~\eqref{eq:ladder} is
already the full band-matrix equation, and pairs inside the coherence
window
\begin{equation}
|a_{\vk n}-a_{\vk m}|<n_{\rm coh}\,
(\tilde\Gam_{\vk n}+\tilde\Gam_{\vk m}),
\qquad n_{\rm coh}=\texttt{--coherence-window}\ (\text{default }6),
\end{equation}
are dressed at their pair-midpoint energy
$\bar\eps=\tfrac12(a_n+a_m)$ (or $0$ in \texttt{fermi} mode); outside
the window the $G^R_nG^A_m$ pair weight is negligible and the bare
element is kept.  Two remarks: (i) the \emph{rails} remain
band-diagonal --- rail-off-diagonal vertex components are suppressed by
$\tilde\Gam/(|a_n-a_m|+\tilde\Gam)$, the same parameter that controls
\textsc{LinReTraCe}'s own neglect of inter-band scattering after the
rotation, so both truncations degrade together (near degeneracies) and
are mutually consistent; (ii) inter-band \emph{scattering} is fully
retained in the rung through $|\delta V^{\rm band}_{nm}|^2$ --- it
contributes to the rates \eqref{eq:rates} and mixes bands inside the
ladder.

\section{Combined mode with \texttt{--dmft}}
\label{sec:dmft}
Static Hermitian parts add before the rotation:
$\tilde H(\vk)=H(\vk)+\Sigma^0_{\rm DMFT}+\mathrm{dc}
+\Sigma^{(1)}_{\rm dis}(\vk)-\mu_{\rm DMFT}$, solved as the generalised
eigenproblem with $Z^{-1}_{\rm DMFT}$ exactly as in
\texttt{wannier\_dmft} ($\Sigma^{(1)}$ is $\vk$-dependent, hence added
per $\vk$).  The rails of both the rate equation~\eqref{eq:rates} and
the ladder~\eqref{eq:ladder} carry the \emph{total} renormalised width
$\tilde\Gam=Z(\Gam^0_{\rm DMFT}+\Gam_{\rm dis})$ and spectral weight
$Z$ (coherent part only, consistent with \textsc{LinReTraCe}); the rung
remains disorder-only (no $e$--$e$ vertex, by the same assumption as in
the core).  Consequently $\lambda$ acquires a parametric temperature
dependence through $\Gam_{\rm DMFT}(T)$ and is computed per
temperature; the DMFT temperature axis takes precedence over
\texttt{--disorder-temps}.  Note there is \emph{no Matthiessen rule for
the vertex}: rates add on the rails, but rungs of different channels
are not additive --- the ladder is solved once with the combined
ingredients.  The dressed energy file carries the first temperature's
vertex (\textsc{LinReTraCe} reads a single energy file; per-$T$ dressed
files are future work, and a warning is issued).

\section{Inelastic rates: three consistent routes}
\label{sec:inelastic}
Phenomenological rates of non-disorder origin ($e$--$e$, $e$--ph) are
\emph{inelastic}; they broaden the rails, suppress the diffuson
enhancement, and provide the Cooperon dephasing cutoff
(Sec.~\ref{sec:cooperon}).  Three ways to include them, in decreasing
order of consistency:
(i) \texttt{--gamma-add C0 C2}: $\Gam_{\rm inel}(T)=C_0+C_2T^2$
[eV, eV/K$^2$] enters the rails of the rate equation \eqref{eq:rates}
\emph{and} of the ladder \eqref{eq:ladder}, is written into the
scattering file per temperature step, and cuts the Cooperon --- fully
consistent; the vertex becomes $T$-dependent (use
\texttt{--dressed-per-t});
(ii) core-side \texttt{ScatteringCoefficients} /
\texttt{QuasiParticleCoefficients} in the \texttt{linretrace} config
\emph{together with} a \texttt{ScatteringFile}: a small core extension
(part of this patch series) makes them \emph{additive} on top of the
file per temperature step,
$\Gam_{\rm add}(T)=\sum_i c_i T^{\,i-1}$ ($T$ in K) and
$1/Z_{\rm tot}=1/Z_{\rm file}+1/Z_{\rm cfg}-1$, applied before the
$\Gam\cdot Z$ convention --- the kernels are then consistent, but a
dressed energy file constructed \emph{without} this rate carries a
ladder with too-narrow rails (acceptable for
$\Gam_{\rm inel}\ll\Gam_{\rm dis}$);
(iii) \texttt{ScatteringOffset}: constant, added and multiplied by $Z$
in the unmodified core (verified: \texttt{input.f90}), same consistency
caveat as (ii).

\section{The maximally crossed (Cooperon) channel}
\label{sec:cooperon}
With \texttt{--vertex-channels cooperon|both} the interface adds the
weak-localisation channel.  The $n$-rung maximally crossed fan at fixed
total momentum $\mathbf Q=\vk+\vk'$ is a transfer operator on the
$\vq_p$ orbit (transplanted from the full transport code, where its
momentum routing was validated against explicit ring traces to
$10^{-16}$),
\begin{equation}
O_{(u),(u-\vq_p)}=\sum_c w_c\big[\delta V_c(u,u')\,G^{R}(u')\big]
\otimes\big[\delta V_c(\mathbf Q{-}u,(\mathbf Q{-}u){+}\vq_p)\,
G^{A}((\mathbf Q{-}u){+}\vq_p)\big],
\end{equation}
with $\delta V_c(\mathbf Q{-}u,(\mathbf Q{-}u){+}\vq_p)
=\delta V_c((\mathbf Q{-}u){+}\vq_p\to\mathbf Q{-}u)^\dagger$ by
hermiticity.  The fan sum $n\ge2$ is $O^{2}(1-O)^{-1}$ ($n{=}1$ is
already contained in the diffuson), closed per $\vk$ in the
$(\vk,\mathbf Q{-}\vk)$ block by the twisted boundary; the result is
cast as an \emph{additive} current vertex through
$t_\vk=\Tr[v^{\alpha}G^{R}C^{\beta}G^{A}]$, i.e.\
$C_{il}(\vk)=P_{il}/G^{A}_{l}(\vk)$ with
$P_{il}=\sum_{jk'}G^{A}_{j}(\mathbf Q{-}\vk)\,X_{(ij)(k'l)}\,
v^{\beta}(\mathbf Q{-}\vk)_{k'j}$, summed over all $\mathbf Q$ of the
mesh.  Dressing then proceeds through the same one-sided pipeline,
$\tilde M=\mathrm{Re}[\overline{v^{\alpha}}(\lambda v^{\beta}
+C^{\beta})]$; the interface reproduces the full-code crossed transport
function to $7\times10^{-16}$ on identical rails (test~5).

\paragraph{Cutoff physics and the emergent regulator.}
The Cooperon pole at $\mathbf Q\to0$ expresses perfect constructive
interference of time-reversed loops; \emph{elastic} disorder does not
dephase it --- its rail broadening cancels against the rung at
$\mathbf Q=0$ by the same Ward identity \eqref{eq:ward} that creates the
pole.  The infrared cutoff must therefore be \emph{inelastic}: with
rails carrying $\tilde\Gam=Z(\Gam_{\rm el}+\Gam_{\rm inel})$ and the
rung elastic-only, $1-O(\mathbf Q{\to}0)\approx
\Gam_{\rm inel}/\Gam_{\rm tot}$ emerges automatically --- the
\texttt{--gamma-add} (or DMFT) rate \emph{is} the dephasing,
$L_\phi=\sqrt{D\tau_\phi}$.  The interface refuses to run the Cooperon
without an inelastic rate (unregularised pole) and issues quantitative
warnings when: the $\mathbf Q$-mesh is coarser than $1/L_\phi$
(recommending $N_i\ge|\mathbf b_i|L_\phi$ --- otherwise the \emph{mesh}
truncates the weak-localisation logarithm); the elastic mean free path
$\ell$ exceeds the supercell linear size (periodic disorder replicas
then interfere coherently; minimal supercell size $\gtrsim\ell$); or the
ensemble is small/asymmetric (the pole region amplifies ensemble
noise).  The crossed contribution is intrinsically $T$-dependent via
$\tau_\phi(T)$; use \texttt{--dressed-per-t} and run
\texttt{linretrace} once per temperature.  Cost: one small orbit solve
per $(\mathbf Q,\text{energy bin})$; the $\mathbf Q$ loop is
parallelised with the pipeline's \texttt{tile\_map}
(\texttt{DWANN\_NUM\_WORKERS} / \texttt{DWANN\_BACKEND=mpi}), the same
rationale as the full scheme.

\section{Outputs and file formats}
\label{sec:outputs}
\textbf{Scattering HDF5} (FullScattering layout, identical to the DMFT
interface): \texttt{.quantities/*} (axes), and per spin prefix
\texttt{step/NNNNNN/scatrate|qpweight|bandshift}, shapes
$(n_k,n_b)$ per temperature step.
\textbf{Dressed energy HDF5}: byte-copy of the plain energy file with
\texttt{momentsDiagonal} $(n_k,n_b,n_{\rm dir})$ and the in-window rows
of \texttt{kPoint/*/moments} replaced by Eq.~\eqref{eq:dressing};
attributes \texttt{disorder\_dressed} (mode) and
\texttt{disorder\_archive}.  Run \texttt{linretrace} on this file plus
the scattering file.  When the dressing is $T$-dependent (combined mode
or \texttt{--gamma-add}), \texttt{--dressed-per-t} writes one file per
temperature (suffix \texttt{\_T\#\#\#\#}); attributes record channels,
evaluation modes, $T$, and \texttt{gamma\_add}.
\textbf{$\lambda$ HDF5} (\texttt{--lambda-output}):
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{7.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
dataset & shape & content \\
\midrule
\texttt{lambdaDiagonal} & $(n_k,n_b,n_{\rm dir})$ &

\section{Usage}
\begin{verbatim}
# 1. plain energy file on a q_p-commensurate, Gamma-centred mesh
lwann <w90folder> --kmesh 6 6 6 -o lrtc-wann.hdf5
# 2. disorder input (rates + dressed elements + lambda analysis)
lwann <w90folder> --kmesh 6 6 6 -o lrtc-wann.hdf5 \
    --disorder deltaH.h5 --disorder-engine scba \
    --disorder-temps 100 500 9 --disorder-temps-log \
    --vertex-eval onshell \
    --scat-output lrtc-dis-scat.hdf5 \
    --dressed-energy-output lrtc-wann-dressed.hdf5 \
    --lambda-output lrtc-lambda.hdf5
# combined with DMFT (its temperature axis takes precedence):
lwann <w90folder> --kmesh 6 6 6 --dmft w2d_T*.hdf5 --disorder deltaH.h5 ...
# 3. run the untouched core on (dressed energy file, scattering file)
\end{verbatim}
Requires \texttt{DWANNIER\_PATH} (or \texttt{--dwannier-path}).  All
flags: \texttt{lwann --help}; workflow details:
\texttt{documentation/DISORDER\_INTERFACE\_MANUAL.md}.
